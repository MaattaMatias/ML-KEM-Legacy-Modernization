3. Health Checks

Readiness Probe (/ready): Validates external dependencies (database connectivity, cache availability). If dependencies fail, traffic is safely routed away from the instance.

Startup Probe: Protects slow-starting services or pre-launch database migrations by pausing liveness checks until initialization completes.

Distributed Tracing: Instrument endpoints with OpenTelemetry (OTel) to propagate trace and span context across microservices and link directly to error logs.
